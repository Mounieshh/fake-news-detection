About the Project

Combating misinformation with transparency and advanced AI.

Overview

This Fake News Detection System is designed to help users verify the authenticity of news articles. By analyzing text content or URLs, our AI model provides an assessment of whether the information is likely real or fake.

The goal is to promote media literacy and reduce the spread of misinformation in the digital age.

How It Works

Users can input raw text or provide a link to a news article. The system processes this input using Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques and a trained classification model.

The core prediction is powered by a BERT-based model, fine-tuned on extensive datasets of real and fake news. Additionally, we leverage Gemini AI for enhanced reasoning and explanation of the results.
